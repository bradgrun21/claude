## Why pastors don't trust the numbers
- Nobody agrees on what counts (volunteers in attendance? online = views or households?)
- Numbers live in five places (Check-Ins, spreadsheet, usher clipboard, YouTube, email)
- Nobody owns the number — entered late, by different people, or not at all
- Duplicates inflate everything
- History gets edited and nobody knows why

## The Count On It checklist

For every number: **Definition · Source · Owner · Deadline · Location in Planning Center**

### Sunday / weekend
- [ ] In-person attendance by service (adults, kids, students separately)
- [ ] Kids check-ins by room and age group
- [ ] Student attendance
- [ ] Volunteers serving (separate; decide whether they count in attendance)
- [ ] Online attendance (define: peak live, unique viewers, or households)
- [ ] First-time guests (adults and kids)
- [ ] Connection cards turned in
- [ ] Salvations / decisions
- [ ] Baptisms

### Weekday
- [ ] Group attendance (taken in Planning Center Groups, not just a roster)
- [ ] Midweek services, classes, recovery ministries
- [ ] Student and kids midweek nights
- [ ] Prayer meetings and other recurring gatherings
- [ ] One-off events (registered vs. actually attended)

### Next steps
- [ ] Membership / growth track: signed up and completed
- [ ] Baptism signups
- [ ] Group joins and requests answered
- [ ] Serve team signups and how many got placed
- [ ] First-time guest follow-up completed (workflows)

### Volunteers
- [ ] Total active volunteers (served in last 90 days)
- [ ] Scheduled vs. actually served
- [ ] Decline and no-response rate
- [ ] New volunteers onboarded per month
- [ ] Background checks current (kids ministry)

### Promo and communications
- [ ] What was promoted, where, when (stage, email, social, Church Center, print)
- [ ] Registrations per event and which promo channel drove them
- [ ] Email opens and clicks
- [ ] Church Center page views for promoted items
- [ ] "How did you hear about us?" on guest and event forms

### Giving (aggregate only, tight permissions)
- [ ] Weekly total vs. budget
- [ ] Number of givers
- [ ] First-time givers
- [ ] Recurring givers

### Care
- [ ] Prayer requests received and followed up
- [ ] Hospital visits / pastoral care contacts
- [ ] Benevolence requests

### Seasonal
- [ ] Easter, Christmas, fall launch, VBS/camp tracked as separate events for year-over-year comparison

### Trust rules
- [ ] One written definition per number, shared with all staff
- [ ] One place each number is recorded
- [ ] One owner and a weekly deadline per number
- [ ] Last week's numbers locked after the deadline; changes get a note
- [ ] Duplicates reviewed monthly
- [ ] Headcounts go into Planning Center Check-Ins, not on paper

## Monthly health dashboard (retainer)
Green/yellow/red per area, rolled into one score.
- People: possible duplicates, missing email/phone, not in household, inactive 12+ mo
- Lists & Workflows: overdue cards, unassigned cards, stale workflows, ownerless lists, naming violations
- Check-Ins: events without locations, kids missing allergy/medical, guests without follow-up, attendance trend
- Groups: no leader, no attendance in 60 days, <3 members, requests unanswered 7+ days
- Services: teams without leader, unscheduled 6+ mo, decline/no-response rate, open spots
- Registrations: past events still open, no capacity, unpaid balances, missing required info

Processes: monthly health check + one-page report, quarterly review call, owner rule,
naming conventions, request intake form, staff offboarding checklist, pre-season tune-up.
Build path: V1 manual sheet → V2 Planning Center API script (read-only token) → V3 branded dashboard.

## Client zero: Palm Valley pilot (approved)
~8,000 weekly attendance · 2 physical campuses · Spanish congregation and service. Written approval received.
Rules: no real Palm Valley data, people or logo in public content; sample data only. Church documents stay with the
church; generic templates and checklist built on own time/equipment. Log hours by area and campus from day one.

Key design decision: one standard, every number tagged by campus and language (not separate systems).
- People: campus on every profile + "preferred language" field
- Check-Ins: locations per campus, headcount per service incl. Spanish
- Groups: campus + language tags · Services: service types per campus + Spanish
- Lists/workflows built once, filtered by campus/language
- Dashboard: total on top, breakdown Campus 1 / Campus 2 / Spanish underneath

Spanish congregation specifics:
- Duplicate rules for two-surname names (García López vs García) and accents (José/Jose)
- Spanish versions of connection cards and forms
- Guest workflows route Spanish speakers to a Spanish-speaking person; Spanish text/email templates
- Spanish pastor owns the Spanish numbers on the data map

90-day plan:
- Wk 1–2: audit; baseline duplicates, missing campus/language by campus + congregation
- Wk 2–4: definitions meeting (exec pastor, both campus pastors, Spanish pastor); data map w/ campus + language columns
- Month 2: Reset order: campus/language fields → duplicates → check-in → groups → services/teams → registrations
- Month 2–3: weekly dashboard sheet split by campus/language
- Month 3: monthly health report to leadership, a section per campus

Video angles: "How a church of 8,000 cleans up Planning Center", "Two campuses without two systems",
"Your Spanish congregation is missing from your numbers".
