# What sells (researched November 2026)

Etsy publishes no sales figures. Everything here is read from review counts and shop sales totals on
Etsy pages as they appeared in search results, so treat it as a ranking of popularity, and re-check the
niche you are building for - it moves.

## The leaders

| Product type | Signal seen | Sale price seen |
|---|---|---|
| Paycheck / monthly budget planner | ~12.8k reviews on the top listing; another ~10.8k | $1.95 |
| Clinical hours tracker (social work, nursing, therapy licensure) | ~12.4k reviews | $3.49 |
| Etsy fees and profit calculator | ~5.6k reviews | - |
| Small business bookkeeping + inventory bundle | many listings | $9-$20 |
| Profession-specific budget (e.g. nurse paycheck budget) | ~1.6k reviews | $1.73 |
| Hours, pay and timesheet trackers | several dedicated search pages | - |
| Student assignment tracker | 4,000+ listings | - |
| Seasonal planners (travel, party, wedding) | seller blogs only - weak evidence | - |

One budget shop showed 177k sales since 2023. "budget template google sheets" had about 40,500
searches a month against roughly 20,900 listings.

## Patterns worth copying (the pattern, never the design)

- **One profession + hours or money.** A tracker for one profession's licensing hours had about as many
  reviews as the biggest general budget. Narrow beats broad for a new shop.
- **Low price, high volume.** Leaders sit at $2-$4 on a permanent sale from a higher list price.
- **Excel and Google Sheets together** in almost every top title.
- **Dark mode** is named in the title of the top budget listing.
- **Titles are keyword lists**, not sentences: product, platform, method, extras.

## What buyers expect inside

Budget planner: monthly dashboard with planned vs actual, budget by paycheck (weekly / biweekly),
bill calendar, expense log, savings / sinking funds, debt tracker (snowball), yearly overview,
subscription tracker, any-year setup, light and dark.

Hours tracker (clinical, volunteer, timesheet): a log with date / category / hours / supervisor or
site, requirement targets per category, progress to each target, totals by week and month, a
print-ready summary for sign-off.

Seller tools (fees, bookkeeping, inventory): a sales log, fee and profit per order, monthly P&L,
inventory with reorder flags, tax-time summary.

Trackers in general: Start Here tab, one log, one dashboard, dropdowns for categories, progress bars.

## Before building a new niche

Search for its current best sellers and write down: the three most-reviewed listings' titles, their
tab lists, sale prices, and what their reviews praise or complain about. Complaints ("formulas broke",
"hard to set up") are the brief.
