## Description

List what you owe, pick a method, and see the month you will be debt-free.

This tracker works in Google Sheets and Excel. Enter your debts once, choose how you want to pay them off, and log payments as you go. The plan updates itself.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
• Payoff Plan – your debt-free date, time to go, interest still to pay and what the plan saves you
• Three methods – Snowball (smallest balance first), Avalanche (highest rate first) or your own order, compared side by side
• Payoff Schedule – every month until the last debt is gone, with each payoff highlighted
• My Debts – balance now, progress bar, pay order, payoff date and next due date for each debt
• Payment Log – one row per payment; add a statement balance when you want interest counted exactly
• Year Summary – what you paid by month and by debt
• Start Here – plain instructions on the first tab

YOU WILL RECEIVE
• 1 PDF with links to copy the Google Sheets versions
• 4 spreadsheets: light and dark, each blank and with sample data
• Excel (.xlsx) files of the same four

GOOD TO KNOW
• Up to 15 debts, forecast up to 20 years ahead
• Add an extra monthly payment or a one-time payment and see the date move
• Payments from paid-off debts roll on to the next debt automatically
• Works for 0% debts, credit cards, car loans, student loans and more
• No macros, no add-ons, no subscription
• Shown in US dollars; you can change the currency format

WHAT YOU NEED
• A free Google account, or Excel 2016 or later
• Basic spreadsheet skills: typing in cells and choosing from dropdowns

HOW IT WORKS
1. Buy and download the PDF from your Etsy purchases
2. Click the link in the PDF and choose “Make a copy”
3. List your debts, pick a method and start logging payments
