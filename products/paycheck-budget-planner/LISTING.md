## Description

Budget every paycheck, see every bill coming, and know exactly what is left.

This planner works in Google Sheets and Excel. Set it up once, log what you earn and spend, and every tab updates itself.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
• Dashboard – one month at a glance: planned against actual for income, bills, spending, savings and debt
• Paychecks – pick a paycheck and see the bills due before your next payday, plus what is left
• Bill Calendar – due dates and paydays on a monthly calendar
• Budget Plan – twelve months of planned amounts you can adjust month by month
• Transactions – one simple log (date, category, amount)
• Savings Goals – sinking funds with progress bars and a save-per-month figure
• Debt Payoff – balances, payoff dates and snowball order
• Year Overview – the whole year by month and by category
• Subscriptions – every recurring charge, with its real monthly and yearly cost
• Setup and Start Here – plain instructions on the first tab

YOU WILL RECEIVE
• 1 PDF with links to copy the Google Sheets versions
• 6 colour themes: Classic, Neutral, Blush, Sage, Pastel and Dark
• Sample-data copies in Classic and Dark, so you can see it working
• Excel (.xlsx) files of all eight

GOOD TO KNOW
• Paid weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly – it handles all four
• Up to 6 income sources, 16 bills, 16 spending categories, 8 savings goals and 8 debts
• Works for any year – change the year on the Setup tab
• No macros, no add-ons, no subscription
• Shown in US dollars; you can change the currency format

WHAT YOU NEED
• A free Google account, or Excel 2016 or later
• Basic spreadsheet skills: typing in cells and choosing from dropdowns
