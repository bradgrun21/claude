## Description

Your wedding budget, guests, seating, vendors and to-do list in one place.

This planner works in Google Sheets and Excel. Enter your names, date and budget once, then add costs and guests as you go. Every tab updates itself.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
• Dashboard – days to go, budget left, payments coming up, replies, meal counts and your next tasks
• Budget – enter a total and a suggested split fills in; track planned against real cost by category
• Expenses – every cost with its estimate, real price, amount paid, due date and status
• Guest List – replies, meal choices, dietary notes, table numbers, gifts and thank-you notes
• Seating Plan – fills itself in from your guest list, with the meal beside each name
• Checklist – 44 tasks with due dates worked out from your wedding date; add your own
• Vendors – contacts, booked or considering, and what each one costs and is still owed
• Day-Of Timeline – the running order, ready to print for your helpers
• Setup and Start Here – plain instructions on the first tab

YOU WILL RECEIVE
• 1 PDF with links to copy the Google Sheets versions
• 6 colour themes: Neutral, Blush, Sage, Pastel, Classic and Dark
• Sample-data copies in Neutral and Blush, so you can see it working
• Excel (.xlsx) files of all eight

GOOD TO KNOW
• Up to 250 guests, 150 expenses, 30 vendors, 16 budget categories
• Up to 20 tables of 10 on the seating plan
• One row per guest, including plus-ones and children
• Rename any category, meal choice or guest group
• No macros, no add-ons, no subscription
• Shown in US dollars; you can change the currency format

WHAT YOU NEED
• A free Google account, or Excel 2016 or later
• Basic spreadsheet skills: typing in cells and choosing from dropdowns

HOW IT WORKS
1. Buy and download the PDF from your Etsy purchases
2. Click the link in the PDF and choose “Make a copy”
3. Fill in the Setup tab and start planning
