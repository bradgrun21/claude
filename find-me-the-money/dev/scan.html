Screenshot reader test

For Brad only, not part of the app. Pick screenshots of a bank app's transaction list. Everything is read on this device; nothing is uploaded or saved.

Runs the reader on a built-in line of text.

How to score: for each row, tick Right when the amount is exactly right and you'd recognize the store. Under each screenshot, enter how many purchases it missed. Accuracy = rows marked right ÷ (purchases found + missed). Rows tagged "Not a purchase?" or "Transfer?" don't count either way. Goal: 80% or better.
