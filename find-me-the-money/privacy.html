Privacy

Your financial data never leaves your phone. Your paycheck, bills, debts, goals and any bank file you import are stored only in this browser on this device. We don't have a server that receives them, and we can't see them.

Bank files are read on your phone. When you pick a CSV file, it's read by the app right on your device. Only the date, store name and amount of each purchase are kept. The file itself isn't uploaded or stored.

We may count visits. If visit counting is turned on, it records that the app was opened or that a step happened (like "finished setup"), with no cookies and never any money amounts, names or account details.

You're in control. Settings → Delete everything erases your data from this device. Settings → Save a backup file creates a copy only you hold.

Not financial advice. Find Me the Money is an educational budgeting tool. It isn't financial, tax or investment advice.
