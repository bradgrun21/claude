Privacy

Your financial data stays on your phone unless you choose an account. Your paycheck, bills, debts, goals and the purchases you add are stored in this browser on this device.

If you sign in, your plan is saved to your account. Signing in is optional. When you do, your email and your plan are stored with Supabase, our database provider, so the plan can follow you to another phone. Each account can only read its own plan. We use your email only to sign you in. Settings → Delete my account copy removes the saved plan.

Screenshots are read on your phone. When you scan a screenshot of your bank app, it's read by the app right on your device. Only the purchases you check and save are kept: the date, store name, amount and category. The screenshot and the text read from it are never uploaded or stored.

Your name, email and activity. When you save your plan, we keep your first name, your email and whether you want money tips and updates from Brad. We also keep a simple record of what you do in the app and when, like "checked in on payday," "finished a lesson" or "scanned this week's purchases," so we can see what helps people and send emails that fit where you are. That record never includes amounts, store names, bill names or anything else from your budget. We don't sell your information. Unsubscribe from emails anytime with the link in any email, or uncheck the box in Settings. Deleting your account copy in Settings removes your name, email list choice and activity record too.

Reminders are optional. If you turn on reminders, OneSignal, our notification provider, receives your phone's notification address linked to your account ID so it can deliver them. Reminders never include amounts, bill names or anything else from your budget. Turn them off anytime in Settings.

We may count visits. If visit counting is turned on, it records that the app was opened or that a step happened (like "finished setup"), with no cookies and never any money amounts, names or account details. We also count payday check-ins by number (1st, 2nd, 3rd and so on) to see whether people come back. That count never includes amounts, names or anything from your budget.

You're in control. Settings → Delete everything erases your data from this device. Settings → Sign out stops saving to your account. Settings → Save a backup file creates a copy only you hold.

Not financial advice. Find Me the Money is an educational budgeting tool. It isn't financial, tax or investment advice.
