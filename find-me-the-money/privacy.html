Privacy

Plain words, short sections. If anything here is unclear, ask us (Contact, at the bottom).

What we keep. Your plan: your budget, purchases, balances, goals, your daily checklist and your gratitude lines. Until you sign in it's stored only on this phone. When you sign in, it's saved to your account with Supabase, our database provider, so it follows you to another phone. Your plan is private to you and anyone you invite to it. If you share a plan with a partner, you both see everything in it, including each other's gratitude lines. When you save your plan we also keep your first name, your email and whether you want emails from Brad, plus a simple record of what you do and when (like "finished a lesson"). That record never includes amounts, store names or anything else from your budget. We don't sell your information.

Budget Snaps and chat. When you snap a receipt or a bank screenshot, the photo goes to Anthropic, our AI provider, only to read the purchases on it. A recipe photo or a grocery receipt you snap in Meal Math goes the same way, only to read the ingredients or prices (with your pantry's item names, so it can match them, never your prices). Chat messages ("HEB 84.20 groceries") go to Anthropic the same way. Card and account numbers are masked first. We don't store the photos or the chat text: we keep only counts and costs, and the purchases you check and save (date, store name, amount, category). Typing purchases in never uses AI. Anthropic handles it under its Commercial Terms, which don't allow it to train its AI models on what we send. AI can misread, so check before you save.

Reminders. If you turn them on, OneSignal sends the push messages (a bill due tomorrow, and an evening nudge on an unfinished challenge day). OneSignal gets your phone's notification address linked to your account ID. Messages never include amounts, names or anything from your budget. Turn them off anytime in Settings.

Email. Resend sends your sign-in codes and, if you said yes, occasional emails from Brad. Unsubscribe anytime with the link in any email, or in Settings.

Community. If you join our Skool community, we match your email (or a second email you give us in the app) to unlock extra Budget Snaps. Zapier passes us that email and whether you're a free or paid member, nothing else. Skool's own privacy policy covers Skool.

Analytics. GoatCounter counts page views and taps (like "finished setup"). No ads, no selling data, no tracking cookies, and never amounts, names or account details.

Your choices. Settings → Save a backup file gives you a copy only you hold. Settings → Delete my account and data deletes your plan, your account and everything saved with it, from this phone and our database, within minutes. Backups held by Supabase roll off on Supabase's own schedule. Settings → Sign out stops saving to your account.

For adults. The app is for adults 18 and over. Find Me the Money is an educational budgeting tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.
