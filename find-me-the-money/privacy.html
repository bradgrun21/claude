Privacy

Your financial data stays on your phone unless you choose an account. Your paycheck, bills, debts, goals and any bank file you import are stored in this browser on this device.

If you sign in, your plan is saved to your account. Signing in is optional. When you do, your email and your plan are stored with Supabase, our database provider, so the plan can follow you to another phone. Each account can only read its own plan. We use your email only to sign you in. Settings → Delete my account copy removes the saved plan.

Bank files are read on your phone. When you pick a CSV file, it's read by the app right on your device. Only the date, store name and amount of each purchase are kept. The file itself isn't uploaded or stored.

We may count visits. If visit counting is turned on, it records that the app was opened or that a step happened (like "finished setup"), with no cookies and never any money amounts, names or account details. We also count payday check-ins by number (1st, 2nd, 3rd and so on) to see whether people come back. That count never includes amounts, names or anything from your budget.

You're in control. Settings → Delete everything erases your data from this device. Settings → Sign out stops saving to your account. Settings → Save a backup file creates a copy only you hold.

Not financial advice. Find Me the Money is an educational budgeting tool. It isn't financial, tax or investment advice.
